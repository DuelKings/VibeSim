AGENTIC SIMULATION KNOWLEDGE BASE — 04 QUESTION-TO-SIMULATION RECIPES
All recipes inherit 00 workflow, 05 output contracts and 06 guardrails. Required structures, thermodynamic states and methods must be identified. 'Organic vs inorganic' is not the sole distinction: molecular vs periodic modeling and mechanism are often the decisive distinction.

RECIPE 01 — RELAXATION / GEOMETRY OPTIMIZATION
Question forms: 'relax molecule/crystal', 'find stable structure', 'minimize energy'.
Quantity: local potential-energy minimum given a specified charge, cell/boundary condition, functional and constraints; NOT proof of global minimum.
Organic molecule: build initial 3D coordinates, identify Q/M/stereo, xTB optimize; CREST if flexible and lowest-energy conformer matters; ORCA Opt at chosen DFT level; Freq to verify local minimum if stability requested. Return optimized XYZ, final energy, RMS gradient/frequency outcome and conformer selection.
Inorganic crystal: identify specific polymorph/CIF and intended phase; QE relax positions at fixed cell if lattice externally fixed; vc-relax if appropriate bulk lattice permitted to change. Converge plane-wave cutoff/k sampling and force/stress. For slabs preserve vacuum and usually fix bottom layers; report lattice/volume and symmetry changes.
Stop: no initial structural definition; invalid charge/spin; severe overlap/reconstruction; nonconvergence; unsupported pseudopotential.

RECIPE 02 — BAND GAP (PERIODIC ELECTRONIC STRUCTURE)
Question: 'What is silicon bandgap?' or 'electronic band structure'.
Periodic inorganic/organic crystal: QE relax (optional) -> converged SCF -> NSCF dense grid for extrema plus high-symmetry bands path for visualization -> locate VBM and CBM with k points, direct/indirect type, gap in eV. Metals may have zero or overlapping band gap. Semilocal Kohn-Sham gaps are not quasiparticle gaps; GW/hybrid beyond baseline, expensive. Must check magnetic order, SOC, dispersion, cell, functional and k mesh.
Isolated organic molecule: there is no bulk band gap; ORCA HOMO-LUMO molecular orbital gap, fundamental gap (IP-EA) or optical excitation are DIFFERENT properties. Ask or state the chosen proxy. Molecular organic crystals: periodic QE, not isolated molecule ORCA if aiming for solid-state band gap.
Mandatory clarification for user asking 'optical band gap' while requesting just DFT: offer approximate electronic gap vs dedicated excited-state methods.

RECIPE 03 — HOMO / LUMO / MO GAP
Question: HOMO, LUMO, frontier molecular orbitals.
Organic isolated molecule: xTB conformation search if flexible -> ORCA DFT optimization, SCF -> parse occupied/unoccupied orbital eigenvalues with spins; gap = E_LUMO-E_HOMO. Report energy in eV, Q, M, geometry, solvent, functional/basis, spin-specific alpha/beta data. xTB orbital values are screening descriptors, not DFT-validated observables. If excitations asked, use TDDFT; IP/EA use separate ΔSCF states.
Inorganic periodic: HOMO/LUMO is not generally the conventional bulk metric: compute valence/conduction band edge, Fermi level, DOS/band gap with QE. For finite inorganic cluster ORCA molecular orbitals may be appropriate.
Stop/warn: fractional occupations metallic materials, open-shell ambiguous labeling, defect states, severe self-interaction or wrong reference alignment.

RECIPE 04 — ADSORPTION ENERGY (NOT RATE)
Question: 'How strongly does CO bind to Pt(111)?', 'adsorption strength'.
Primary formula: E_ads=E(surface+adsorbate)-E(surface)-E(gas adsorbate), negative = exothermic adsorption for given references. Alternative sign convention E_binding=-E_ads, label explicitly. If molecule dissociates or surface stoichiometry changes, write balanced stoichiometric equation and chemical-potential references instead. Binding energies for molecular pairs below.
Inorganic periodic slab (e.g. Pt): QE clean slab optimize/reference -> independently optimized molecule/reference (compatible method) -> build multiple surface adsorption sites/orientations -> QE relax adsorbed slabs -> SCF energies -> finite-size, coverage, layers, vacuum, k/cutoff, spin, dispersion, dipole, charged-slab corrections as needed. Constrain lower layers and document coverage. Adsorption thermodynamics at T,p uses chemical potentials, ZPE/entropy, possible solvation, not raw E_ads.
Organic surface: if an organic *molecule* on solid surface, still QE periodic slab. If organic molecule binds noncovalently to another organic molecule in gas/solution, ORCA cluster interaction/binding protocol, check conformers and BSSE/solvent. Organic molecular crystals on surfaces are periodic phenomena.
Outputs: all three energy references and geometries, chosen site/coverage, energy vs site, uncertainty; no rate.

RECIPE 05 — ADSORPTION RATE / DESORPTION RATE / UPTAKE KINETICS
Question: 'adsorption rate', 'sticking coefficient', 'how fast binds'.
A single relaxation, E_ads or even one finite AIMD trajectory CANNOT yield a general adsorption rate. Must distinguish: sticking probability per collision, adsorption flux molecules/(area*time), rate constant (units depend on kinetic law), equilibrium uptake/time, surface reaction rate, diffusion-limited sorption.
Required: surface/site, gas or liquid conditions, T, partial pressure/concentration, coverage, mechanism, activation barrier and prefactor or validated kinetic/transport model. For activated surface adsorption: QE energetics plus reaction-path barrier via NEB (separate validated template), thermochemical corrections and transition-state theory when applicable; multiply collision flux by sticking coefficient for gas adsorption; microkinetics/kMC may be needed for coverage dependence. Barrierless molecular sticking may demand properly sampled dynamics, not only static NEB. For adsorption into a porous organic material/polymer, force-field/MD/GCMC or mass-transfer/diffusion model may be more appropriate than small-cell DFT.
Default safe result absent kinetics info: offer adsorption ENERGY only as a screening proxy, explicitly say does not answer requested RATE; mark BLOCKED_MISSING_INPUT or propose bounded staged plan. Require approval for rate-quality workflow.

RECIPE 06 — BINDING ENERGY / INTERACTION ENERGY
Question: 'binding energy' for complex AB or ligand-host.
Define E_bind = E_AB - (E_A+E_B), negative favorable; note positive binding energy conventions elsewhere. For isolated organic supramolecular complex: ORCA compute multiple poses + fragment energies, basis/dispersion, counterpoise if appropriate; distinguish frozen-fragment interaction energy vs fully relaxed binding energy (deformation included). For inorganic bulk/defect/interface: QE formation/association energy via balanced reference species, chosen stoichiometry, charge states and chemical potentials. For organic molecule adsorbing on solid use adsorption protocol not isolated cluster unless explicitly finite cluster model.
Bond dissociation energy: E(products)-E(reactant) with correct fragment spin states, ZPE and enthalpy if requested; not the same as a kinetic barrier.
Stop: missing products, ambiguity about what 'binding' objects are, inconsistent charge/stoichiometry.

RECIPE 07 — BOND BREAKING / BOND DISSOCIATION CURVE
Question: 'what happens if I pull an H-H bond apart?'
1 identify cleavage type homolysis vs heterolysis and molecular charge/spin states; 2 choose reaction coordinate R and physically meaningful fragment states; 3 generate series of geometries (H2 example: H-H distance 0.5 to 5 Å with dense points around equilibrium and dissociation region); 4 ORCA fixed-geometry SP at each distance or constrained relaxation orthogonal to coordinate for larger molecule; 5 plot E(R)-E(reference); 6 independently calculate dissociation products consistent with electronic states; 7 test strong correlation and spin restrictions. DFT restricted closed-shell can give wrong dissociation asymptote for H2; unrestricted broken-symmetry may yield spin-contaminated solutions. For accurate H2 PEC, suitable wavefunction/multireference treatment may be needed (e.g. small-system FCI with adequate basis); not all ORCA installations/workflows make these methods available. xTB scan only screening. Different conformational pathways possible in organics.
Inorganic/periodic: choose bond in crystal/surface and whether adiabatic cleavage leads to defects/desorption; QE constrained displacement/relaxation and NEB if seeking energy barrier. Surface bond cleavage creates finite-size image and charge/spin issues, use cell convergence. For small inorganic gas molecule, ORCA generally better than vacuum QE.
Outputs: distances Å, energies eV or Hartree, proper reference asymptote, force where available, sampled states, ground-state branch assumptions. NEVER call PES slope a rate constant; static scans do not include entropic kinetics.

RECIPE 08 — ACTIVATION ENERGY / REACTION BARRIER
Reactant and product atom-balanced, same total charge/electron count for direct adiabatic path; choose relevant surface/spin states. Molecular organic: ORCA TS optimization/frequency (one imaginary mode with intended reaction coordinate) and connectivity validation (IRC where available), conformers and thermal effects. Periodic inorganic: QE NEB via neb.x with relaxed endpoints and images, convergence/tangent/check barrier; missing saddle or unphysical interpolation invalidates. Barrier ΔE‡ or ΔG‡, not E_bind. Chemical rates require validated prefactor, temperature and kinetics regime. Escalate if unknown mechanism/multiple pathways.

RECIPE 09 — FORMATION ENERGY / DEFECT ENERGY
Inorganic: chemical-potential reservoir choice critical. E_form(defect,q)=E_defect(q)-E_bulk-Σn_i μ_i+q(E_F+reference)+E_corr (sign convention of n_i must be specified); align electrostatic potential, supercell sizes, charge corrections, magnetism, band edges, μ bounds. Not autonomous without declared defect and μ regime. Organic: analogous reaction enthalpies/formation with gas/solvation correction, ORCA balanced species; do not substitute total molecule energy for formation energy.

RECIPE 10 — FINITE TEMPERATURE / AIMD / DIFFUSION
Inorganic QE Born-Oppenheimer MD: state ensemble NVE/NVT/etc, T, pressure, dt (~0.5–1 fs only an initial light-atom-dependent pilot), simulation length, equilibration, independent replicas, cell/functional, compute RDF/MSD/diffusion only after sufficient sampling; short AIMD rarely resolves slow rare events. Organic isolated conformational dynamics: xTB/CREST approximate sampling, not production physical time evolution; reactive AIMD beyond defaults and requires explicit specialist route. Diffusion D from long-time linear MSD/(2d t) in unwrapped coordinates and appropriate regime, not from a short drift.

RECIPE 11 — MOLECULAR CONFORMER RANKING
Organic: build stereochemically specified molecule, xTB opt -> CREST or RDKit+xtb conformer search -> cluster -> shortlist -> ORCA DFT optimizations -> frequencies/solvent where needed. Inorganic: polymorphs, defects and adsorbate sites are structural configuration searches, not molecular torsional conformer search; QE separate candidate relaxations, budget and phase/composition controls. Global-minimum claims need broader evidence.

RECIPE 12 — TOTAL ENERGY / SINGLE-POINT ENERGY
Fixed geometry and electronic state only. Organic ORCA SP at specified charge/multiplicity/method; inorganic QE SCF with periodic cell/PP/XC/k points. Absolute total energy is method- and reference-dependent; comparing different composition, charge or different methods without balanced reaction is meaningless. If asked 'can I scan H2 by repeated SCFs?' yes, the fixed-geometry PES is well-defined within chosen electronic method, BUT spin state/dissociation correlation, charge, wavefunction symmetry and reference validation are essential.

RECIPE 13 — COHESIVE ENERGY / LATTICE ENERGY
Inorganic crystalline cohesive energy: per atom difference between crystal and appropriate isolated atoms with correct spin/atomic reference, beware PP and open-shell atom convergence, method-specific limitations. Molecular crystals lattice energy: periodic cell energy vs isolated molecules same method/reference and dispersion; intramolecular deformation vs intermolecular stabilization must be distinguished. Organic molecular cohesive energies from ORCA gas molecule alone do not capture crystal periodicity.

RECIPE 14 — VIBRATIONS / STABILITY
Organic ORCA Opt+Freq yields harmonic modes, ZPE and finite-T corrections, but check low-frequency and entropy treatment. Inorganic bulk phonons require QE phonon code ph.x/phonon dispersion and supercell or DFPT workflow, distinct from just relaxation; imaginary phonons may signal instability/numerical issues. These are not silently part of normal optimization.

DEFAULT RESPONSE FOR ALL RECIPES: State exact metric, whether calculation is molecule or periodic, software, composition/state, geometry generation, assumptions, input settings and sensitivity, staged commands, validations, outputs, resource threshold and scientifically unsupported claims. Ask clarification only for decisions essential to physical meaning; otherwise do bounded screening with assumptions.
